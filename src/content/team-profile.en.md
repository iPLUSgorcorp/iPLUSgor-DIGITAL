**iPLUSgor Digital is an independent Ukrainian strategy, UX/UI and frontend team building conversion-focused websites for service businesses with existing traffic.**

We connect campaign intent, a clear offer, trust, mobile UX, CTA hierarchy and lead capture in one system. The same team follows the work from review through launch.

**Fewer handoffs. More clarity from paid click to inquiry.**

# Full profile

## Who we work with

Our primary client is a service business already receiving traffic from Google Ads, Meta Ads, organic search or referrals, while the current website does not explain the service or next step clearly enough.

This can include roofing, HVAC and home-service companies, dental practices, medical spas, legal practices and other high-ticket services where a qualified inquiry matters.

## What we change

* align the page with traffic context and the primary offer;
* structure trust, evidence and objection handling;
* define the hierarchy of calls, forms or booking actions;
* design a mobile-first interface;
* implement responsive frontend, forms and analytics-ready events;
* verify the primary journey and prepare launch.

## Accountability

A short timeline is possible only with a clearly fixed scope, available materials and timely decisions. We do not guarantee leads, conversion rate or advertising return: demand, pricing, the offer, media buying, sales and business operations also affect results.

Self-initiated work is labelled as concept work. Commercial work is published only with explicit client permission.
