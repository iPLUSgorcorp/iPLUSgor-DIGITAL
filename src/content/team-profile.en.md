# Full profile

## Who we are

iPLUSgor Digital is an independent Ukrainian team of designers, strategists and frontend developers within the iPLUSgor design company. The division focuses on digital modernization for manufacturing, engineering, technology and B2B companies.

We create corporate websites, product catalogues, configurators, administration panels, analytics systems, AI assistants and other digital tools required by a modern business.

Our specialization is especially relevant to companies with complex products, large catalogues, dealer networks, technical documentation and long decision-making cycles.

These may include manufacturers of industrial equipment, heating, ventilation and lighting systems, pumps, construction materials, steel structures, energy and climate technology, or other engineered solutions.

## How the iPLUSgor brand is structured

iPLUSgor was founded in Ukraine in 2026 as a young design company. We are at the beginning of our journey, earning trust through careful work, honest communication and responsibility for the result. iPLUSgor Digital is responsible for websites, digital platforms, UX and frontend. iPLUSgor Creative operates as a separate creative territory for cover design, graphic editing, visual content and video editing.

We began with private digital projects while developing our capabilities in web design, frontend development, automation and proprietary digital systems.

Over time, we identified a systemic problem: many strong companies have quality products, experience, production capabilities and a solid reputation, yet their digital presence remains in the past.

Their websites are often complex, overloaded and difficult to use on mobile devices. They fail to explain product value and force customers to work through dozens of pages, tables and PDF files on their own.

That is why we chose to focus not on producing another attractive website, but on modernizing a business’s digital infrastructure.

We want the quality of a company’s digital presence to match the quality of its product.

## How we work

Our process consists of five main stages:

### 1. Audit

We analyze the existing website, structure, catalogue, content, competitors, mobile experience, performance, navigation logic and user journey.

### 2. Structure

We form a new information architecture and structure for pages, categories, products and user scenarios.

### 3. Design

We create the visual concept, UX/UI design, design system and responsive versions of key pages.

### 4. Development

We turn the design into a fast, responsive and technically considered frontend. We connect the necessary systems, integrations, analytics and administration tools.

### 5. Launch

We verify the platform, migrate content, correct critical scenarios, launch the website and prepare it for further development.
