# About iPLUSgor Digital

**iPLUSgor Digital is an independent Ukrainian web design and frontend team creating and modernizing clear, contemporary websites for businesses.**

We take a project from the initial business review and structure through experience and interface design, responsive frontend development and launch.

After handover, the client remains in control: the domain, infrastructure accounts and source code belong to the client or are transferred to them. We do not create artificial technical dependency on our team.

We are a young team earning trust without invented numbers, awards or clients. Self-initiated concepts are always identified as concepts rather than presented as commissioned work.

**We modernize the surface. We preserve the core.**

# Full profile

## Who we are

iPLUSgor Digital is an independent Ukrainian team for websites and digital platforms. The team works across business review, structure, experience and interface design, frontend implementation and launch.

Responsibility inside the team is organized by function:

* client and product direction — sales, discovery calls, product concept, design direction and oversight of the result;
* lead generation and outreach operations — organization of client-search systems and CRM;
* initial communication — cold outreach, telephone calls and first contact with potential clients.

We do not claim an unverified production-team size, a long company history or global-agency status. The exact production setup follows the task, while responsibility for the outcome stays with iPLUSgor Digital.

## Who we work with

Our primary client is an established small or medium-sized business that needs a new website or a substantial improvement to its current one.

This can include:

* furniture and kitchen companies;
* construction and renovation businesses;
* construction and renovation businesses;
* local shops;
* service companies;
* clinics and dental practices;
* education centres;
* other businesses whose website must explain the offer and support enquiries.

We focus on websites that present a local business clearly, explain its services and help people get in touch.

## What the client receives

Before production, we review the business and existing website, discuss goals, requirements, materials and references, and define the structure that is actually needed.

A core project may include:

* website structure and user journeys;
* UX and visual concept;
* UI design;
* desktop and mobile adaptation;
* frontend development;
* placement of supplied content;
* basic launch preparation;
* connection of the client’s domain;
* website launch.

Additional pages, multilingual support and needed integrations are scoped separately when they genuinely support the business.

## Control and ownership

After the agreed handover is complete, the client controls the website.

* the domain belongs to the client;
* hosting and infrastructure accounts belong to the client or are transferred to them;
* the source code is handed over to the client;
* continued work with iPLUSgor after launch is not mandatory.

Post-launch support can be arranged separately according to project needs.

## How a project works

### 1. Initial contact

We briefly determine whether the company needs a new website or redesign and whether the task fits our way of working.

### 2. Discovery call

We learn about the business, services or products, requirements and preferences. Before the call, the client may prepare materials, brand guidelines, a list of required pages and examples of websites they like.

### 3. Scope

We define the website structure and the exact project composition.

### 4. Design

We create the visual concept and principal screens.

### 5. Client review

We present the work and gather consolidated revisions.

### 6. Development

We turn the approved design into a working responsive frontend.

### 7. QA

We verify desktop and mobile behavior, links, forms and key scenarios.

### 8. Launch

We connect the client’s domain and publish the website.

### 9. Handover

We transfer access and source code within the agreed project scope.

## Timeline and pricing

The timeline is defined after the initial review and depends on scope, page count, content readiness and required functionality.

We first determine what the business actually needs, then provide a clear scope and project price. We do not publish a universal starting price that ignores the real task.

## Evidence without invention

Commercial work is published only with the client’s permission. Self-initiated redesign studies and interface concepts are explicitly labelled **Independent concept — self-initiated study** and are never presented as commissioned projects.

While our public client-case library is developing, we show what can be inspected directly: before/after concepts, UX reasoning, responsive interfaces and working frontend demonstrations.
