# Process imagery

The four self-hosted WebP images were generated with the built-in image tool and optimized to 1280 × 854 pixels. They are illustrative editorial scenes. They do not show I+Gor staff, actual customers, client work or measured outcomes. The website states this alongside the images.

The revision replaced staged desk imagery and fabricated screen interfaces with quieter documentary-style scenes. The prompts asked for ordinary workplaces, natural light, imperfect framing, believable human posture, muted color and no readable invented text, logos, floating graphics or fake dashboards.

| File | Scene requested |
| --- | --- |
| `diagnose.webp` | Service-business operator reviewing intake notes and a paper calendar at a practical desk. |
| `design.webp` | Two people discussing a hand-drawn workflow on a large paper sheet. |
| `build.webp` | Developer working at one laptop beside a paper system sketch, with the code out of focus. |
| `deploy.webp` | Two operators checking a phone and printed launch checklist at a service counter. |

These scenes explain the Diagnose → Design → Build → Deploy sequence. They must not be described as documentary proof of I+Gor projects.
